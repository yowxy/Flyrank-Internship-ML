# Week 1 — What I'm Proving (+ Sitemap)

## Proof statement

I ship honest machine-learning prototypes on messy, real-world data: small
models with their limits written down, like my content-refresh queue that
beats a hand-rule baseline and says exactly where it fails. I'm proving this
to a hiring manager at a small tech team who needs a junior that delivers a
working prototype instead of a polished demo, so they will email me to set
up a chat.

**Why this page needs to exist:** a CV lists "Python, scikit-learn" exactly
like ten thousand other CVs. It can't show how I caught a leaky feature,
why I picked a client-holdout split, or what my model got wrong — this site
shows the working and the limits, not the tool names.

## Narrowing trail (why this version, not the broader ones)

- Rejected: "I do AI, data analysis, and machine learning." → three skills
  hiding behind "and", describes anyone.
- Rejected: "I build ML models on real data." → no honesty angle, no person,
  no proof anyone can check.
- Kept: one verb (ship honest prototypes), one person, one action, and one
  checkable proof (the refresh queue) that only matches my repo.

## Why each page earns its place (and what got cut)

| Page | Job against the claim + action | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Passes the 30-second test: claim, one proof teaser, email CTA | Keep |
| Work | The actual proof: FlyRank refresh queue, baseline-vs-model, leakage demo | Keep |
| About | Answers "who is this guy" in 4 lines so the claim has a face | Keep |
| Contact | The one action lives here: email + CV + GitHub, nothing else | Keep |
| Blog | No regular writing habit yet; an empty blog proves the opposite | Cut |
| Services/pricing | I'm looking for a job/chat, not selling packages | Cut |
| Testimonials | None yet; fake quotes would kill trust | Cut |

## Pressure-test (run in the workspace, full log in `ai_workspace_spec.md`)

Verdict on the map: Home, Work, and About each earn their place; **Contact
as a standalone page is wasted** — a page with one button and three links
adds a click between belief and the action, which is exactly where visitors
drop off.

**One thing I'll change:** fold Contact into the Home page (final section)
and the site footer. Sitemap goes from 4 pages to 3: Home (with contact
block) → Work → About. The one action gets closer, not farther.
